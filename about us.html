About Spectrum Discovery Center

Spectrum Discovery Center was founded with a simple vision: to create a science museum where every child feels welcome, included and inspired to learn.

The idea for Spectrum grew from a personal experience. The founder has a daughter with autism and often found that many attractions and learning environments were not designed with different sensory and learning needs in mind.

Spectrum was created to be different. We believe that every child deserves the opportunity to explore, discover and learn in a way that works for them.

Thats why we have designed spaces with quiet zones, sensory-friendly areas, soft lighting and inclusive activities that support different learning styles and abilities.

Our mission is to make science accessible, engaging and meaningful for everyone, from curious young explorers to families, schools and special education groups.

At Spectrum, learning happens through play, creativity and hands-on experiences.

We celebrate curiosity, encourage discovery and welcome every visitor exactly as they are.
